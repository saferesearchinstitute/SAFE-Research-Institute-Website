Evidence Library

The SAFE Research Institute publishes research-based educational resources and policy analyses, making peer-reviewed analysis accessible to the public on a non-discriminatory basis. These materials are intended to educate scientists and the public about how evidence can be communicated effectively in public policy settings; they are not intended to encourage lobbying or to advocate for the adoption of any particular legislation.

About the Foundation

During its initial years, the Foundation operates primarily through volunteer support. Activities are conducted virtually using secure digital collaboration tools. Foundation policy is that publications are reviewed by the Scientific Advisory Board prior to release.

The Foundation is affiliated with the Science and Freedom for Everyone Action Fund ("SAFE Action"), a separate legal entity organized under Section 501(c)(4) of the Internal Revenue Code. The two entities maintain separate governance, separate bank accounts, and separate financial records. The Foundation does not engage in political campaign activity and does not use its funds to support the political or legislative activities of SAFE Action. Any administrative costs shared between the two entities are allocated based on documented relative use and reimbursed at fair market value.
